# Competitive positioning signal — 2026-11-28

## What surfaced

Public search surfaced Complyra's free product compliance checker. Its page says a seller describes a product, destination, category and optional materials; it identifies likely regulations and supplier documents across 47 listed regulations and several markets, without requiring an account. The vendor positions the checker inside a procurement/RFQ and supplier-sourcing platform. This is a direct substitute for a generic “tell me what regulations and documents may apply” message.

Source: https://www.getcomplyra.com/tools/compliance-checker

## Marketing implication (hypothesis, not validated user research)

TraceWize should not lead only with “check regulations” or “get a supplier document list.” Those messages are already occupied by a free competing checker. The stronger claim to test is the handoff around a real seller decision: organize the exact product/model, origin and destination, sales channel and fulfillment route, then identify what the seller still needs to confirm with the supplier, platform, broker/carrier, or specialist before buying or shipping.

Before making this a public differentiator, verify that the current live flow actually collects and returns those elements consistently; do not claim that TraceWize has broader market coverage, more complete legal data, or higher accuracy than the competitor without evidence.

## Recommended next experiment

Use one short seller-facing demonstration/sample, not another broad promise: show a generic Bluetooth accessory through (1) exact model and evidence identity, (2) one selected destination and sales/fulfillment route, and (3) three questions assigned to supplier, platform, or broker. Invite a small seller to complete the real screen and say what next action it changed. Measure completed screens, not impressions alone.
