# Three-seller pilot — 15-minute moderated test

Purpose: validate whether a small electronics seller can use the Can I Sell It pre-screen to organize a real product-and-destination decision. This is a usability/demand test, not a compliance review or consulting engagement.

## Who to invite

An owner/operator who currently sells or is preparing to sell a powered, wireless, connected, or battery-containing product into the United States. Prioritize a small seller who is responsible for sourcing/listing decisions. Do not count compliance consultants, brokers, labs, or other service providers as seller validation.

## Invite text (LinkedIn DM or email, only after the seller opts in)

Thanks for offering to test TraceWize. It’s an early-stage product-and-route pre-screen—not an approval or legal/customs opinion. Would you be willing to spend about 15 minutes trying it with one product you sell or plan to sell to the U.S.? Please don’t share supplier names, confidential reports, customer data, or private commercial figures. I’m interested in where the workflow is unclear and whether the result changes what you would verify next. No endorsement is expected.

## Run of show

1. **Context (2 min):** What product type do you sell/plan to sell? Are you already selling to the U.S., or deciding whether to start? What triggered the check (supplier quote, platform notice, shipping plan, or pre-purchase decision)?
2. **Unassisted task (5–7 min):** Ask the seller to open https://tracewize.com/can-i-sell-it.html and try one product/route. Say “please think aloud”; don't coach unless they are stuck. Note which fields they hesitate on, skip, or misunderstand.
3. **Result check (3 min):** Ask what they believe the result tells them, what it does not tell them, and what they would verify with a supplier, marketplace, broker, or specialist.
4. **Value (3 min):** Ask whether they would use it again before a specific decision they actually face. Which part saved time or reduced uncertainty? Which part was not useful? What would they have done without it?

## Questions that reveal real value

- Did this test change a real next step, or was it only interesting?
- What would you do next with the result, and who would you contact?
- Would you have paid for help with this decision before this test? What did you actually spend last time (only if they choose to share)?
- Would you be willing to run another product or refer a seller with a similar decision? This is stronger evidence than “sounds useful.”

## Record without over-collecting

Record only: seller role (owner/operator), broad product class, route, sales stage, whether they completed the task, time/hesitation point, misunderstood phrase, next action they named, and whether they volunteer a follow-up/referral. Use a short pseudonym or “Seller 1/2/3.” Do not store confidential source docs, SKU/model values, supplier identities, customer data, or price/revenue details. Don't record the call or capture the screen unless the participant separately agrees.

## Success signal

Count each seller as validated only when they complete one real product/route screen and give concrete feedback. Separately report:

- invited / agreed / attended;
- completed task;
- actual decision or next step affected;
- asked for another use or referred a peer;
- any stated willingness to pay (not the same as purchase).

Don't call a like, connection, polite compliment, or hypothetical interest a trial. A single person's experience is still a qualitative signal, not proof of market demand.
