## Today's original post — DRAFT, NOT PUBLISHED

> Before you commit to a new electronics SKU, make the product identity easy to trace.
>
> Ask your supplier to put these details on the quote or product sheet—not just in chat:
> • exact model / part number and hardware revision
> • what is included in the retail package
> • battery chemistry and whether the battery is built-in, replaceable, or sold separately
> • wireless functions and the markets you plan to sell into
>
> Then check that the same identifiers appear on the label, manual, and any test or safety documents you receive. A report for a similar-looking product is not automatically evidence for your exact configuration. If something differs, flag it for the lab, platform, or qualified specialist to assess; don't infer compliance from a matching brand or product photo.
>
> The right requirements depend on the exact product and market. The CPSC's Online Sellers' Safety Guide is a useful U.S. starting point for small importers and online sellers: https://www.cpsc.gov/Business--Manufacturing/Online-Sellers-Safety-Guide
