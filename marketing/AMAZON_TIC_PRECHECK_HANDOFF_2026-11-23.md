# Before you send a battery-product file to an Amazon-authorized TIC provider

## A practical evidence-matching worksheet

Use this to organize your own records before contacting the provider named or selected through the relevant Seller Central request. It is not an Amazon checklist, test plan, certification decision, legal opinion, or promise that existing reports will be accepted.

### 1. Anchor the request to the exact marketplace case

- Amazon store / destination: ____________________
- ASIN(s) in the notice: ____________________
- Account Health notice date and case/reference number: ____________________
- “Address issue by” date or stated response deadline: ____________________
- Product listing name as shown in the affected store: ____________________

### 2. Match each ASIN to the product that was actually tested

For each ASIN, record—not infer—the product details shown on the packaging, label, manual, and report:

- Manufacturer / brand and legal name: ____________________
- Exact model, part, or item number: ____________________
- Battery pack or cell model and manufacturer (if listed): ____________________
- Battery chemistry and configuration (if known): ____________________
- Included battery, adapter, charging base, or other shipped component: ____________________
- Differences between ASIN variants, bundles, or revisions: ____________________
- Any mismatch or unknown that needs the supplier to clarify: ____________________

Keep source files unmodified. Do not change model identifiers, dates, results, or manufacturer details to make files appear to match. Ask the provider what documents and format it will review for the specific notice.

### 4. Separate the next actions

- **Supplier/manufacturer:** confirm the exact shipped configuration and resolve any model/battery mismatch.
- **Seller:** monitor Account Health, preserve the notice, map each ASIN to its exact configuration, and submit the provider request according to Amazon’s current flow and deadline.
- **Amazon-authorized TIC provider:** determine whether existing documents meet the applicable Amazon policy or whether more evidence/testing is needed.
- **TraceWize:** at most, help organize product facts, destinations, and evidence questions before that handoff; it cannot determine TIC acceptance, change a deadline, clear a listing, or appeal an Amazon decision.

### 5. Ask the provider before commissioning repeat work

1. Can you review these existing files for the ASIN/model/configuration in my notice?
2. What exact report versions, model matches, or other evidence are missing for your review?
3. Do you need a product sample, and what is the intake process and expected timeline?
4. If you recommend new testing, which requested evidence gap would that test address?
5. What is the next Seller Central step and deadline after you receive the documents?

## Important boundary

Amazon’s U.S. direct-product-validation guidance says existing documents may be submitted to an Amazon-authorized TIC provider for review. The provider decides whether they meet the current policy; neither this worksheet nor TraceWize can pre-approve that decision. Follow the notice and current Seller Central instructions for the affected ASINs.
