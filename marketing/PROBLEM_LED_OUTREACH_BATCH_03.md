## 1. Ok_Serve_6985 — “grey scheme” DDP from China to the US

- Community: `r/Alibaba`
- Source: https://www.reddit.com/r/Alibaba/comments/1x7e3j9/cheap_ddp_shipping_from_china_to_the_us_can_the/
- Timing when found: approximately 8 hours old.
- Decision stage: buyer is considering whether to proceed with a low-cost DDP offer after the factory described it as a “grey scheme.”
- Community constraints: self-promotion must remain below 10%; no DM solicitation; generic AI lead-generation content can result in a ban.

Proposed public reply:

> The clearest red flag is that the provider itself called the arrangement a “grey scheme.” I would not assume that being absent from the entry automatically insulates your company. Whether liability could reach the buyer is fact-specific, but your communications and knowledge of the arrangement may still matter, and successful delivery does not make the underlying entry permanently safe from review.
>
> Before proceeding, require the proposed entry structure in writing: the legal name of the Importer of Record, the licensed customs broker, the HTS classification, country of origin, customs value, duty calculation, bond, and whether you will receive the CBP entry summary after clearance. Ask whether the declared value will match the real commercial transaction. If they refuse to identify those parties or say the value will differ from the invoice, do not use that route.
>
> Because you are asking about potential legal exposure, have an independent U.S. licensed customs broker or customs attorney review the written proposal before the goods ship. I would not rely on the factory or its forwarder to define the limits of your risk.

## 2. Web_N0mad — China-to-UAE LCL/DDP documentation and import VAT

- Community: `r/SmallBusinessUAE`
- Source: https://www.reddit.com/r/SmallBusinessUAE/comments/1wxapqi/suggestions_regarding_ddp_sea_cargo_forwarders/
- Timing when found: approximately 5 hours old.
- Decision stage: VAT-registered UAE business is selecting an LCL structure and needs documentation sufficient for accounting and input VAT treatment.
- Community constraints: no spam, self-promotion, cold pitches, DM solicitation, or service offers outside the designated megathread.
- Source check: the UAE Federal Tax Authority defines the importer as the person listed as importer at customs clearance and instructs registered importers to account for import VAT through the applicable import/VAT process.

Proposed public reply:

> The key issue is not the DDP label; it is whose name, customs code and TRN the import is actually recorded against. The UAE FTA defines the importer as the person listed as importer for customs-clearance purposes. If an LCL consolidation is cleared under the forwarder’s own setup, do not assume that the forwarder’s commercial invoice or cost breakdown will be enough for your company to support input VAT treatment.
>
> Before choosing a provider, ask for a redacted sample document pack from a comparable completed shipment: the customs declaration/bill of entry showing the importer field, duty and VAT calculation, evidence of the TRN or customs code used, freight invoice, and a document that links your cartons and commercial invoice to that entry. Then have your UAE accountant or tax agent confirm in writing whether that exact pack is sufficient for your VAT return.
>
> If the forwarder cannot show how the import and VAT will be linked to your company’s TRN, compare the total cost with DAP or another structure using your own customs broker. It may look more expensive, but it gives you a document trail in your company’s name instead of discovering the accounting problem after arrival.

Official references checked:

- UAE FTA glossary: https://tax.gov.ae/en/content/glossary.aspx
- UAE FTA VAT Import Declaration User Guide: https://tax.gov.ae/DataFolder/Files/Pdf/VATImportersUserGuideEnglishV050.pdf
