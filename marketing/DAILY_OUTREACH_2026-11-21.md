> Selling a regulated consumer product to U.S. buyers one parcel at a time? A low shipment value does not automatically remove the CPSC eFiling step.

> CPSC says that when a product requires a certificate, the certificate must be eFiled regardless of shipment value—there is no Section 321 (de minimis) exemption for eFiling.
>
> Before relying on a low-value or direct-to-consumer shipping setup, check:
> • Is this exact finished product subject to a CPSC rule that requires a certificate?
> • Who is handling the certificate data and entry message with the customs broker?
> • Does the product identifier in the certificate match the item being shipped?
>
> This is not a claim that every electronic product needs a CPSC certificate. First determine whether the product is in scope; then plan the filing workflow. CPSC's FAQ: https://www.cpsc.gov/FAQ/eFiling-Frequently-Asked-Questions-FAQ
