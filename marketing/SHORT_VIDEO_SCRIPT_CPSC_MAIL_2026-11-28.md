# 45-second short-video script — CPSC international mail (cross-post option)

**Status:** Script only; not recorded or published. Use on LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram Reels, or YouTube Shorts only if Carey chooses. Check official guidance on the posting date.

**0–4 sec — On-screen:** “Shipping a regulated product to the U.S. by international mail?”

**4–13 sec — Spoken:** “CPSC says certificate data for covered mail shipments that already require a certificate will be eFiled starting January 20, 2027.”

**13–26 sec — Spoken:** “But ‘international mail’ does not automatically mean the parcel qualifies for Entry Type 13. Ask your broker or carrier whether your shipment needs formal entry or qualifies for the Entry Type 13 test.”

**26–36 sec — Spoken:** “And eFiling changes how certificate data is submitted—it doesn’t make every electronic product require a CPSC certificate.”

**36–45 sec — Spoken / on-screen CTA:** “I made a one-page checklist to help organize the questions. TraceWize is a preliminary screen, not certification or customs advice. Sources and checklist in the caption.”

**Caption:**

For a specific shipment, confirm the product's certificate scope and the actual mail-entry path with your broker or carrier. CBP rule: https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2026-10-22/html/2026-21600.htm · CPSC current guidance: https://www.cpsc.gov/eFiling-Document-Library · Preliminary screen: https://tracewize.com/can-i-sell-it.html

**Accuracy guardrails:** Keep “covered products that already require a certificate”; don't say every electronic item needs a CPSC certificate. Don't describe Entry Type 13 as automatic. Distinguish the temporary Entry Type 13 test allowance from the CPSC certificate-data requirement. Never show customer or supplier confidential information.
