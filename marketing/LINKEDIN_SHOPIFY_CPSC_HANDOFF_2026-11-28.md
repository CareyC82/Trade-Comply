# Future LinkedIn post — Shopify CPSC data handoff

Status: research-backed draft only. Do not publish as a second post today. Recheck Shopify/CPSC guidance on the day of publication.

## Copy

If your Shopify product needs a CPSC certificate, a saved registry ID is only one part of the import handoff.

Shopify says CPSC eFiling data is stored at the product level, not the variant level. Shopify stores the reference IDs you enter, but does not file them with CBP for each shipment. Its help page also says Shopify Shipping carriers currently can't provide CPSC eFiling references to brokers at the U.S. border.

Before the next shipment, check:
• Does this exact finished product require a CPSC certificate?
• Do variants share the same certificate/reference IDs, or do some need separate handling?
• Who will send the applicable data to the broker or carrier, and who will transmit it with the entry?

This is a data handoff question, not a new testing rule. CPSC eFiling changes how certificate data is transmitted; it does not make every product subject to certification.

Shopify guidance: https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/international/cpsc
CPSC scope and dates: https://www.cpsc.gov/Business--Manufacturing/Business-Education/Business-Guidance/Certificates

TraceWize is an early-stage product-and-market pre-screen; it does not decide product-specific legal obligations or file customs data.

For Shopify sellers, which handoff is less clear: matching a model to its certificate, or getting the right data to the broker?

## Accuracy notes

- Shopify's statement about variant-level data is specific to Shopify's product-disclosure feature; it does not establish that the underlying CPSC certificates are variant-identical or different.
- Product scope must be determined based on the applicable CPSC rule/standard and the actual product. Do not generalize to all electronics or battery-powered products.
- Ask rather than assume the seller's broker/carrier workflow. Shopify says its Shipping carriers currently cannot provide the reference data to brokers at the U.S. border; do not extend that claim to all carriers or third-party shipping arrangements.
