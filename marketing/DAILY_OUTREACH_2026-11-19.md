## Today's original post — DRAFT, NOT PUBLISHED

> Selling electronics on eBay to U.S. buyers? Two FCC dates are easy to mix up.
>
> **October 13, 2026** is when the FCC's marketplace-liability provisions took effect. eBay says it may ask for an FCC ID on selected listings through the Issue Resolution Center; it is not asking every seller to add one to every applicable listing on that date.
>
> **June 1, 2027** is the point-of-sale display deadline for a narrower group of listings: new devices requiring FCC certification, offered by high-volume third-party sellers, with stated exclusions for used devices, smaller sellers, and certain existing listings.
>
> Either way, a platform timeline is not the same thing as the underlying product rule. If eBay asks about a specific listing, follow the notice in your Issue Resolution Center and match any FCC ID to the exact product and model—never a similar item. An FCC ID also isn't blanket approval for every product requirement or marketplace.
>
> eBay's current seller guidance explains both dates and the listing-specific process: https://www.ebay.com/sellercenter/resources/fcc-id
