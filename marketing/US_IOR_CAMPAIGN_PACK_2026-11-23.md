## LinkedIn post — draft for next publishing window

**A U.S. mailing address—and “DDP”—do not answer the most important import question: who is actually the Importer of Record?**

CBP's new rule for covered U.S. entries for consumption takes effect January 5, 2027. It requires a non-CTPAT foreign IOR to designate a licensed, CTPAT-validated broker to make entry. CBP's rule currently says only Canadian foreign IORs are eligible for CTPAT validation. In a specified power-of-attorney situation, the broker must act as IOR if its principal is not the owner or purchaser.

Before your next shipment, get written answers to four questions:

1. What legal entity and IOR number will appear on the entry?
2. Who files the entry, and is that broker licensed and CTPAT-validated under the exact registered name?
3. Who will be listed as IOR for this shipment?
4. Will it enter for consumption on or after January 5, including if it's already in transit or stored in a bonded facility?

I made a one-page preflight checklist to help organize that conversation. It is not legal advice or an IOR-eligibility determination; confirm your specific route with a licensed customs broker or CBP.

Source: CBP's interim final rule, 91 FR 69977: https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2026-11-06/html/2026-22735.htm

## LinkedIn follow-up post — FBA DDP vs. IOR (draft only)

**For Amazon FBA imports, “DDP” and “who is the IOR?” are two different checks.**

Amazon requires FBA shipments to arrive with duties and charges paid, and its guidance says Amazon will not be the Importer of Record. But that does not, by itself, tell a seller which entity is named as IOR on a particular entry or which broker files it.

For entries clearing in the U.S. on or after Jan. 5, 2027, a foreign IOR that is not CTPAT-validated must designate a licensed, CTPAT-validated broker. A specific POA/principal situation can also require that broker to act as IOR.

Before the next shipment, ask the forwarder or SEND provider for the written answer to three separate questions: Who is named as IOR? Who files the entry? What is the broker's CTPAT status under its registered name?

This is a handoff checklist, not a legal determination. Confirm the specific shipment with the broker and platform.

Sources: Amazon [FBA import requirements](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub/reference/external/G201468520?langue=en_US); [CBP rule](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2026-11-06/html/2026-22735.htm).

**Title:** Before paying the deposit: five product facts to match for a rechargeable Bluetooth product

**Audience:** Small consumer-electronics sellers, Amazon FBA importers, sourcing/operations staff.

**15-minute format:**

1. Pick one non-confidential example product and write down exact model/variant and legal manufacturer.
