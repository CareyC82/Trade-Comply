## Today's original post — DRAFT, NOT PUBLISHED

> Importing a consumer product into the U.S.? One important distinction in CPSC’s new eFiling process:
>
> **eFiling changes how required certificate data is sent—not which products need a certificate.** If a finished product is already subject to a CPSC rule, ban, standard, or regulation requiring a certificate, most imported products have been subject to eFiling since July 8, 2026. Products entered from a Foreign Trade Zone for consumption or warehousing have a later effective date: January 8, 2027.
>
> Before a shipment, ask:
> 1. Does this exact product fall under a CPSC requirement that calls for a certificate?
> 2. Who is responsible for the certificate data, and how will it reach CBP’s ACE entry—through a full PGA message set or a reference to data in CPSC’s Product Registry?
> 3. Do the product identifiers and certificate details match the goods and shipping documents?
>
> Don’t assume every electronic product needs a CPSC certificate—or that eFiling alone determines product applicability. Check the product-specific rule and coordinate the filing workflow with your customs broker.
>
> CPSC’s eFiling FAQ: https://www.cpsc.gov/FAQ/eFiling-Frequently-Asked-Questions-FAQ
