# LinkedIn draft — Amazon FBA is not the importer of record (Dec. 3 option)

**Status:** Draft only; not published. Verify Amazon and CBP guidance again before posting.

## Copy

Sending imported inventory to an Amazon U.S. fulfillment center? Don’t assume Amazon is your importer of record.

Amazon says it will not act as IOR for FBA inventory. The seller must arrange the importer and customs entry directly with its broker or freight forwarder.

That matters even more before January 5, 2027. CBP's new foreign-IOR rule applies to entries filed on or after that date—including goods already in transit. A foreign entity acting as IOR must meet the rule's conditions; a non-CTPAT-validated foreign IOR must designate a licensed broker that is CTPAT-validated to make entry on its behalf.

Before shipping, ask:
• Which legal entity will be named as IOR?
• Who is the licensed broker filing the entry?
• If the IOR is foreign, has the broker confirmed that the arrangement meets CBP’s new rule?

An FBA destination or a DDP quote alone does not answer those questions. Confirm the exact arrangement with your broker.
