**Status: not sent.** A possible no-cost New Zealand small-business education route. The site says it reaches businesses with 19 or fewer employees through its website, newsletter and social channels, but accepts informational content only (no advertising/product placements), requires a credible expert and national relevance. Its guidelines allow links only to freely accessible material without required signup or advertising.
