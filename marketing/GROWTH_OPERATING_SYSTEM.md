# TraceWize Growth Operating System

Started: 2026-10-20

## Operating rule

Do not pause the pipeline while waiting for replies. Research, community participation, LinkedIn outreach, email outreach, and follow-up run in parallel. Review results once per week instead of after every individual contact.

## Weekly throughput

| Activity | Weekly target | Quality rule |
|---|---:|---|
| Decision-stage community replies | 12-15 | Answer the exact problem; no brand mention or DM trap in the first interaction |
| LinkedIn connection requests | 15-20 | Only people whose role or current activity matches a TraceWize use case |
| Personalized emails | 10-12 | One observable business trigger and one concrete risk question per email |
| Follow-ups | 8-12 | Add a new observation, checklist, or sample; never send a generic bump |
| One-page sample reports | 2-4 | Built from a real product-route-market scenario |

## Parallel lanes

1. Product-market access: electronics, batteries, adapters, toys, smart-home products, and marketplace documentation.
2. Import structure: DDP, importer of record, valuation, tariff, VAT/GST, and customs-document chains.
3. Route expansion: US, UK/EU, Australia, New Zealand, and selected high-signal cases elsewhere.
4. Cross-border payment: only when tied to an actual trade transaction, supplier, jurisdiction, or document flow.

## Funnel stages

1. Useful public interaction.
2. Prospect voluntarily shares a product/route/document problem.
3. TraceWize returns a one-page risk snapshot.
4. Prospect confirms whether the findings changed a sourcing, testing, shipping, or launch decision.
5. Offer a paid deeper review only after that value has been demonstrated.

## Metrics

Track weekly:

- qualified touches;
- public replies and private replies;
- conversations containing a real product, route, or document;
- sample reports requested and delivered;
- meetings or pricing questions;
- paid conversions;
- which problem statement produced each response.

## Two-week decision rule

Do not judge a channel before at least 40 qualified touches. If response is low, change the audience/problem framing. If conversations occur but no one shares inputs, reduce the requested commitment. If samples are requested but do not lead to next steps, improve the sample and offer rather than increasing message volume blindly.
