# LinkedIn post draft — recruit three electronics sellers for a usability test

Status: Draft only. Proposed Dec. 7 slot; do not publish without Carey review. This is a user-research invitation, not a promise of compliance advice or a paid service.

## Draft

I’m looking for three small online sellers who sell (or are preparing to sell) consumer electronics across borders.

I’m building TraceWize, an early-stage tool that gives a preliminary product-and-market pre-screen. I’d like to watch a seller try it with one product and one destination market, then hear where the result is unclear or not useful.

The test takes about 15 minutes. No purchase, no fee, and no need to share supplier contracts, confidential test reports, customer data or other sensitive documents. Please use only information you are comfortable sharing. This is a usability test of an early-stage tool, not a compliance determination or tailored legal/customs advice.

If you’re an independent electronics seller and would be open to a short test, comment “seller test” or send me a LinkedIn message. I’ll share the details and confirm the product/market fit before scheduling.

TraceWize: https://tracewize.com/can-i-sell-it.html

## Carey review checklist

- Confirm that a 15-minute session can be personally hosted and that the test flow works on the day of publication.
- Confirm candidate market is among current coverage, and do not imply every destination/product is equally covered.
- Avoid asking participants to upload confidential supplier or customer information.
- Record only explicit participant consent and test outcome; do not count an expression of interest as a completed test.
- If a participant expects a professional compliance opinion, clarify the scope before scheduling.
