# LinkedIn draft — Australia seller research on supplier evidence

**Status: draft only — not published. Suggested slot: Dec. 5 or next open daily slot.**

Australian small businesses are not simply failing to ask suppliers for documents.

In a 2026 ACCC-commissioned survey of 400 Australian small businesses dealing with consumer products, 85% said they ask suppliers for product-safety evidence. Among those businesses, 67% said they always verify supplier claims, and 97% keep some form of compliance record.

The friction is more specific: 25% of surveyed businesses cited difficulty relying on suppliers or manufacturers for accurate safety information. Among businesses that sell products from overseas, that rose to 31%, versus 15% among those that do not.

That suggests the useful question is not just “What documents should I request?” It is “Does this evidence describe the exact model and version I’m buying, and what still needs confirmation for the destination and route?”

TraceWize is an early-stage product-and-market pre-screen for consumer electronics. It organizes product identity, selected market/route and unresolved evidence questions; it does not certify products or replace testing, regulators or specialist advice.

ACCC research: https://www.productsafety.gov.au/about-us/publications/product-safety-research-small-business-report

For sellers taking products overseas, where does the evidence handoff most often break: exact model, test scope, label, or importer/shipping route?

## Fact notes

- ACCC/Bastion surveyed 400 Australian small businesses with fewer than 20 employees dealing with consumer products; fieldwork ran May 27–June 15, 2026.
- 85% asking suppliers and 67% always verifying use different denominators as stated in the report; 97% retaining records is among the suppliers-evidence-asking businesses.
- The 31% vs. 15% comparison is for difficulty relying on supplier/manufacturer accuracy, split by whether the business sells products from overseas. Do not present this survey as electronics-only or claim that 31% is a TraceWize conversion estimate.
- Recheck the public TraceWize page before posting and use only as an illustration of the current workflow, not as evidence of efficacy.
