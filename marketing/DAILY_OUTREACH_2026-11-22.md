## Today's original post — DRAFT, NOT PUBLISHED

> Buying a wireless product from an OEM? A supplier’s “same FCC ID” answer may not tell you whether the production version still matches the authorized configuration.
>
> Before approving a component substitution or design change, ask the supplier:
> • What changed from the configuration covered by the FCC grant—radio module, antenna, RF circuitry, enclosure, or transmitter software?
> • Which exact model/configuration does the grant cover, and who is the responsible grantee?
> • Has the grantee or its TCB documented whether the change is permitted under the FCC change rules, or whether an updated filing is needed?
>
> Don’t assume every change requires a retest or new FCC ID. But don’t rely on a generic certificate when the shipped configuration may differ. FCC rules put continuing conformity responsibility on the responsible party and provide specific pathways for permitted changes.
>
> Sources: 47 CFR §§ 2.931, 2.932, 2.1043: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/chapter-I/subchapter-A/part-2/subpart-J/section-2.931
