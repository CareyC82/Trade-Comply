## Copy

Shipping a CPSC-regulated product to the U.S. by international mail? Put January 20, 2027 on your broker-check list.

CBP delayed certain postal-entry requirements to that date. It does **not** make Entry Type 13 the automatic path for every mail parcel: depending on eligibility, formal entry or the Entry Type 13 test may apply.

Separately, CPSC says covered mail shipments that already require a certificate will need certificate data filed through a Full or Reference PGA Message Set from January 20. That changes the filing handoff—not which products need a certificate. CBP has temporarily allowed incomplete PGA data in the Entry Type 13 test; don't treat that test flexibility as a permanent waiver.

Before dispatch, ask your broker or mail provider:
• Does this exact product require a CPSC certificate?
• For this parcel, is formal entry required or is it eligible for the Entry Type 13 test?
• Who transmits the certificate data and checks the exact product/certificate match?
