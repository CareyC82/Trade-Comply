## High-intent problems found

### EU low-value e-commerce Product Identifiers (PIDs)

The EU PID requirement became mandatory on 2026-11-01 for in-scope imported distance sales. Commission guidance scopes it to consignments with intrinsic value no more than EUR 150, mainly B2C; B2B flows are in principle not in scope. The declaration uses M-PID and NS-PID at minimum, and S-PID if it exists. If no standardized identifier exists, Y081 is a customs declaration code—not a barcode/GTIN to invent or automatically type into an eBay UPC field. PID fields must be repeated for each product within a declaration item/bundle.

Sources:

- European Commission announcement: https://taxation-customs.ec.europa.eu/news/product-identifiers-e-commerce-imports-mandatory-1-november-2026-2026-11-03_en
- European Commission guidance, section 8.7 and codes C127/C128/C129/Y081: https://taxation-customs.ec.europa.eu/document/download/053e5b4e-f0be-4f20-9a23-3e3b659a6676_en?filename=Guidance_5_October_2026.pdf
- eBay seller guidance and channel-specific eIS/SpeedPAK/self-ship flows: https://pages.ebay.com/eu-product-identifiers/
- Shopify Help Center mapping and variant-level workflow: https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/international/shipping/eu-product-identifiers

### Urgent US importer-of-record transition: Jan. 5, 2027

An official CBP interim final rule was published Nov. 6 and takes effect Jan. 5, 2027. For covered entries for consumption, a non-CTPAT foreign IOR must designate a CTPAT-validated licensed customs broker to make entry; under a specified power-of-attorney condition, that broker must act as IOR. A broker being licensed alone is not enough. CBP states that only Canadian foreign IORs are currently eligible for CTPAT validation, so Chinese sellers should not be told that their foreign entity can simply choose to become CTPAT-validated. The rule also creates a U.S.-IOR qualification/declaration process; an American mailing address alone does not establish status. The rule reaches covered entries for consumption on/after Jan. 5, including withdrawals from bonded warehouse/FTZ. CBP says brokers are expected to be fully compliant by May 6, 2027, though some may request delayed compliance. Since it is an interim final rule and comment deadline is Jan. 5, 2027, re-check official updates before publishing date-sensitive guidance.

Amazon Seller Forums published a practical seller-facing summary six days ago. A new seller question asks whether Amazon SEND + UPS shipments automatically meet the rule; no safe answer can be inferred from the public question. The useful first checks are the actual named IOR on entry paperwork, who files, the broker's CTPAT status, and written confirmation from Amazon/SEND or the customs broker for that route.
