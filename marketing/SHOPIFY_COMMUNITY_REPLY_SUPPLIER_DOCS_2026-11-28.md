# Shopify Community reply draft — supplier documents for EU electronics sellers

**Status: draft only — not posted.**

Target thread: [Documents to ask your suppliers for if you sell into the EU](https://community.shopify.com/t/documents-to-ask-your-suppliers-for-if-you-sell-into-the-eu/724077)

## Suggested reply

One extra point for electronics: keep each document tied to the exact product identity you plan to list and ship—not just a supplier’s broad product family. I’d ask the supplier to identify the manufacturer and exact model/variant, then check that the model named in each applicable declaration or test report matches the item, radio/battery configuration, and revision you’ll actually receive. If a report covers only a related model, ask the supplier or lab to explain the scope instead of assuming it carries over.

For an EU online offer, GPSR Article 19 separately calls for visible manufacturer contact details, the EU responsible-person details when the manufacturer is outside the EU, product-identification information, and applicable warnings/safety information. That listing layer is distinct from the technical evidence kept for the product. The exact CE/DoC and test requirements still depend on which product-specific EU rules apply; this isn’t one universal document pack.

I’d also record what is still missing rather than treating “CE certificate” or one test PDF as a blanket approval. A short model-by-model evidence index can make supplier follow-up much easier.

## Why this is tailored and safe

- It adds electronics-specific model/variant matching to the OP’s supplier checklist instead of repeating it wholesale.
- It distinguishes online-offer information under GPSR Article 19 from technical evidence and avoids claiming that GPSR creates one universal electronics certificate.
