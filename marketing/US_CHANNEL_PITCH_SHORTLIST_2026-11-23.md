eBay says Seller Circles are seller-led meetings; its host requirements include U.S. residence, an eBay selling account in good standing, and at least $10,000 in sales during the past 12 months. Carey’s profile currently shows Hong Kong, so do not apply as a host. Instead, ask an existing chapter organizer only whether a short practical guest Q&A is welcome. Potential educational session: **“Before you commit to another electronics order: five facts that help a supplier or specialist review your product.”** Use a generic charger or Bluetooth-speaker example; distinguish exact model/configuration, battery/power variant, destination/channel, existing evidence, and seller-vs-supplier action. Official references: [eBay events](https://community.ebay.com/events/), [Seller Circles directory](https://community.ebay.com/sellercircles/), [host requirements](https://community.ebay.com/about-groups/).

### 2. Free interview channels — qualify before applying

- **Winning With Shopify:** Its page advertises free guest slots and invites founders, app makers, and agencies. It is a potential educational exposure test, but the show is Shopify-specific and its public page is UK-based; the audience reach figures are publisher claims, not independently verified. Carey should be transparent that TraceWize is early-stage and avoid presenting user outcomes that do not exist. Application page: https://wwspodcast.com/pages/podcast-speakers .
- **The eCommerce Podcast:** It accepts founder stories and practical experts, but its application says submission subscribes the applicant to a newsletter and requires agreeing to Terms & Conditions. Do not submit until those terms/subscription are separately reviewed and approved. An alternative is a direct pre-inquiry asking whether an early-stage founder story is a fit and whether there is any cost: https://www.ecommerce-podcast.com/be-a-guest .
- **eCommerce Edge:** No guest fee is stated and it seeks B2B commerce leaders, manufacturers, distributors, and service-provider experts. This audience is more adjacent/partner-facing than small marketplace sellers, so lower priority for direct seller acquisition: https://www.greenwoodconsulting.net/podcast-guest-application .
- **Shopify1Percent:** The current page welcomes self-nominations, says it does not charge guests, and describes a Shopify merchant audience. However, its editorial criteria emphasize a specific story, proof or firsthand data, and a lesson merchants can use; the stated audience/reach figures are the publisher’s own claims. TraceWize has no verified seller outcomes yet, so do not pitch a generic founder/company overview now. Revisit after 3–5 real testers provide non-confidential examples of what changed in their sourcing decision. https://www.shopify1percent.com/suggest-a-guest/ .
- **Product Startup:** Free appearance is advertised and the audience is hardware founders, but the current page says “No episode promotion” and the latest visible episodes are dated 2024; the current status and exposure are uncertain. Not a near-term channel absent confirmation that the show is actively producing. https://talks.co/productstartup/ .
- **Do not pursue paid placements yet:** EcomCrew currently says guest appearances have an appearance fee; Ecommerce Coffee Break says plans start at $249 and presents the package as an advertorial/media placement. No fee is authorized. Sources: https://my.ecomcrew.com/media-kit-podcast-guest and https://ecommercecoffeebreak.com/podcast-guest-application/ .
- **Do not accept blanket recording-rights release without approval:** Selling From the Beach asks for an irrevocable right to record, distribute, edit, and create derivative works from the interview, plus a waiver regarding use of likeness/voice. It may be free of appearance fee, but the rights are broad: https://www.sellingfrom.co/podrequest .

This may be a better low-risk discovery opportunity than cold pitching local groups, but the format is year-end sharing, so attend as a listener and do not inject a commercial pitch. - Group fit is mixed: the NYC circle describes both new/experienced sellers and a broad assortment of used and new goods; eBay seller-circle directories are not electronics-importer lists. Use organizer opt-in, and qualify the audience before proposing a product-specific session.
- Amazon already offers free Seller University resources and describes Seller Assistant as covering compliance; TraceWize should **not** compete on “Amazon compliance training” or promise account-health case resolution. More defensible positioning is cross-market/product-specific pre-buy organization: exact model/configuration, destination, supplier evidence, and what can or cannot be transferred across markets. Amazon itself positions Seller University as free educational content across seller operations, and advertises Seller Assistant as including compliance: https://sellingpartners.aboutamazon.com/seller-university .
- Read-only Amazon Seller Forums research surfaced examples of sellers reporting RF-attribute confusion, unavailable category attributes, or opaque documentation prompts. This is anecdotal demand/problem discovery, not an endorsement, prevalence measurement, or proof that TraceWize can resolve a marketplace appeal. No forum activity was performed.
