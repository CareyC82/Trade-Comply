## If someone responds — prepared factual replies (use only when relevant)

- **“Does every international-mail parcel need Entry Type 13?”** Not automatically. The shipment must qualify for that test path; formal entry may be the applicable route. Ask the broker/mail-entry provider how the specific shipment will be filed.
- **“Do all electronics need a CPSC certificate?”** No. CPSC eFiling changes the way data is submitted when a certificate is already required; it does not create a certificate obligation for every electronic product. Confirm the product-specific rule and intended use.
- **“Will Shopify/Amazon file this for me?”** Don’t assume. Platform help, seller-account document requests, and customs entry filing are distinct workflows; confirm the exact role with the platform and the customs broker/carrier.
- **“What does TraceWize do?”** It is an early-stage pre-screen that organizes product/destination facts and questions to verify before supplier, platform, broker, or specialist review. It does not certify a product, file entry data, guarantee marketplace approval, or replace a licensed professional.

Avoid resolving individual product classification, legal eligibility, or filing advice in comments. Link the official source and direct the commenter to the responsible broker/agency for case-specific confirmation.
